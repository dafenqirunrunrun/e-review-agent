# V1.6.1.11 Pilot Dataset Role Freeze

Status: `PILOT_DATASET_ROLES_FROZEN`

Amazon Reviews 2023 is frozen as an ecommerce compatibility corpus and risk-candidate discovery source only. It is not a labeled risk benchmark.

ASAP Chinese Reviews is frozen as a Chinese expression and sentiment auxiliary corpus. It is excluded from the ecommerce risk taxonomy denominator, is not multimodal, and is not a formal risk benchmark.

Formal evaluation still requires explicitly authorized and annotatable ecommerce review data.
